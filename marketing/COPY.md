# Scorched Tanks: Marketing Copy

Don't call the game "Scorched Earth" or say it's official. It's a tribute to the classic DOS artillery games.

## Name and tagline
- Name: **Scorched Tanks**
- Tagline (EN): Aim. Fire. Scorch the earth.
- Tagline (HE): לכוון. לירות. לשרוף את האדמה.

## Short description (Play Store, max 80 chars)
- EN: Classic turn-based tank artillery. Play online with friends and draw your own maps.
- HE: ארטילריית טנקים קלאסית בתורות. משחקים אונליין עם חברים ומציירים מפות.

## Full description (EN)
Remember the tank duels on the family DOS PC? Scorched Tanks brings them back on your phone, with a fresh coat of paint.

- 2 to 10 tanks, any mix of humans and CPUs (7 AI personalities)
- Over 30 weapons: Nukes, MIRVs, Funky Bombs, Napalm, Rollers, Diggers, Sandhogs, Dirt, Lasers and more
- Shields, parachutes, guidance systems and fuel, bought one unit at a time between rounds
- Wind, walls, gravity, sky and terrain options
- Play online: quick match with strangers, or a private room with an invite link
- Map builder: draw the ground with your finger, add trees, bushes and old wrecks, then play it online too
- A modern hand-painted look (sunset skies, grassy hills, burning trees), or the original Classic look at one tap
- Touch-first controls, plus save and continue

No ads. No accounts. Just artillery.
